# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is local clients in Mannheim and the Rhein-Neckar area who are comparing beauty and laser treatments and want a calm, trustworthy way to decide before booking. The current content and service mix suggest women are the main audience; this is inferred from the supplied materials and remains open to client confirmation. The site must also remain welcoming to men and all suitable adult clients.

## Product Purpose

The website introduces S&O Beauty Salon, explains its treatments and technology, establishes local trust, and converts interested visitors into personal WhatsApp appointment enquiries. Success means a visitor quickly understands what is offered, why the studio is credible, where it is located, and how to ask for the right treatment.

## Positioning

S&O combines personal treatment planning in Mannheim's Q1 quadrate with Soprano ICE Platinum laser technology and NiSV-qualified operation. The promise is individual guidance and a calm, professional treatment experience rather than a high-volume menu or automated booking funnel.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually arrive from Google, Google Maps, Instagram, referrals, or a shared link. They browse on mobile, compare one or two treatments, look for evidence of hygiene, technology, competence, location, and a clear process, then contact the salon through WhatsApp. Appointments are agreed personally rather than through a booking engine.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Main services: Laser-Haarentfernung, AquaFacial, Microneedling, Wimpernlifting, and professionelle Hautpflege.
- Primary conversion: WhatsApp appointment enquiry at +49 15565 855752.
- Location: Q1, 7, 68161 Mannheim.
- Opening hours: Monday to Friday, 10:00-18:00.
- The visible website must not publish a treatment price list; the client requested its removal.
- Claims about comfort or results must stay qualified and must not promise medical outcomes.
- The site must work well on mobile first, remain fast with video, and preserve local SEO fundamentals.
- Legal-page business details are still subject to final client verification.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: S&O Beauty Salon.
- Preserve the supplied interlocking S&O mark and the soft blush, ivory, and warm neutral cues from the existing identity.
- The experience should feel fresh, minimal, premium, personal, and credible rather than flashy or template-like.
- Real treatment material should be preferred over generic stock imagery.
- Booking language should remain simple, personal German using the formal "Sie" voice.

## Product Principles

1. Let real evidence earn trust before making claims.
2. Make the next step personal and obvious without pressuring the visitor.
3. Explain treatments in plain German and set realistic expectations.
4. Treat mobile speed, legibility, and local findability as core product quality.
5. Prefer a short, considered experience over repeated sections and decorative UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable type, strong contrast, keyboard-visible focus, reduced-motion support, captions or descriptive alternatives for meaningful video, and language that does not assume one gender or promise one universal treatment response.
